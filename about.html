About GLCL

A labour company built from the quayside up.

Get Labour Company Limited was established in July 2013 in Tema, Ghana, to connect businesses with suitable workers — and workers with real opportunities.

01 Who we are

Workforce support for port, cargo-handling and beyond.

GLCL is a Ghana-based labour supply and workforce support company. We provide skilled and non-skilled personnel — including port-related and cargo-handling operations — for businesses that need people they can depend on.

The company is run from Meridian Road, Tema, close to the port it serves. What started with hands-on experience on the quayside still shapes how GLCL works today: know the job, know the people, and never oversell either.

02 Purpose

Mission and vision.

Two sentences that keep the company pointed in one direction.

Mission

Connect businesses with suitable workers

To connect businesses with suitable workers through responsive, reliable and needs-based labour supply — and to support employment opportunities for people seeking work.

Vision

A trusted provider of workforce support

To be a trusted labour provider recognised for dependable workforce support and professional service delivery.

03 Values

Six words we try to earn daily.

Reliability, professionalism, integrity, efficiency, safety and responsibility — the standards GLCL holds itself to on both sides of every placement.

04 People

The approved staff directory.

The people responsible for requests, allocation, finance, safety and transport. This is a directory, not an org chart — every role supports the same promise to businesses and workers.
