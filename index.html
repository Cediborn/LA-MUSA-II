02 What we supply

Skilled and non-skilled labour, matched to the request.

Roles are matched to what the job actually needs — not pushed into a standard package. See the full service breakdown.

Skilled Labour

Qualified operational personnel for machinery, supervision and record-keeping roles. Relevant academic records and certificates help us place skilled applicants appropriately.

  • Forklift operators
  • Reach stacker operators
  • Crane operators
  • Supervisors / foremen
  • Drivers / heavy-duty drivers where requested
  • Tally, record and delivery clerks

Non-Skilled Labour

Reliable general manpower for the physically demanding side of port and transit operations. Applicants must be fit for the work requested.

  • General labourers
  • Ashore labour
  • Transit labour
  • Topside labour
  • Onboard-gang labour

03 How it works

From request to deployment, in three steps.

A simple, human process — a real person reviews every request that comes in.

  1. Submit your labour needs

    Send the roles, category, site and contact details through the request form, or call the office directly.

  2. GLCL reviews and identifies personnel

    We check the request against our registered workforce and identify workers suited to the roles and conditions.

  3. GLCL follows up with you

    We confirm availability and arrangements with your team so the crew arrives prepared for the work.

Please note: GLCL aims to respond to labour requests within 24–48 hours. This is a response aim, not a guarantee of placement or fulfilment.

04 On site

People first. Protective equipment as standard practice.

Port and cargo-handling work carries real risk, so the way personnel are briefed, equipped and supervised matters as much as the headcount. GLCL works with recognisable protective equipment — helmets, high-visibility wear, goggles, ear protection, safety boots and gloves.

  • Personnel briefed for the site and the role before deployment
  • Protective equipment expected in line with site requirements
  • Supervision and foremen available where a job calls for it
