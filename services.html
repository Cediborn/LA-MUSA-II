Services

Labour matched to the job, not to a template.

GLCL supplies both skilled operational personnel and non-skilled general labour for port, cargo-handling and supporting work. Every request is reviewed individually.

01 Skilled labour

Qualified personnel for machinery, supervision and records.

Skilled applicants are asked to provide relevant academic records and certificates so that placements reflect what each person is actually qualified to do. Roles are confirmed against the request before deployment.

    Request skilled labour

    02 Non-skilled labour

    General manpower for the demanding side of the job.

    Non-skilled applicants must be physically fit for the work requested. Gangs are assembled to the size and shape of the task, whether that is ashore work, transit handling, topside work or an onboard gang.

      Request non-skilled labour

      03 How roles are matched

      A request goes in. People come out the other side.

      There is no price list or standard package — labour is scoped to what the job needs, then matched against the workforce GLCL has registered.

      1. Scope the request

        Roles, category (skilled, non-skilled or both), site and timing are captured from your team.

      2. Match against the register

        Allocators identify suitable personnel — checking certificates where a skilled role is involved.

      3. Confirm and follow up

        Availability and arrangements are confirmed with you, with follow-up through the operations team.

      Response aim: GLCL aims to respond to labour requests within 24–48 hours. Response time is not a guarantee of placement, and pricing is discussed per request rather than published.

      Have a role to fill?

      Send the details through the request form and the operations team will review them.
